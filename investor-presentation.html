How It Works

Three steps. Zero friction.

1

Browse & Order

Students open the app, browse the live menu, add items to cart, and place their order in under a minute.

No login required to browse. Personalized after sign-in.
2

Pay & Wait

A QR code is generated at checkout. Students scan to pay and immediately receive live order status updates.

Real-time Supabase subscriptions power instant push notifications.
3

Pickup & Enjoy

Staff marks the order as ready. Student gets notified and walks to the station to collect — no wait, no queue.

Station-routing ensures orders reach the right prep counter.
